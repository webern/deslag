Thank you for your interest in contributing to Suyimazu :)

To contribute to Suyimazu you can do the following:

- Submit new games or updates to the [library](https://codeberg.org/Alexander88207/Suyimazu/src/branch/main/Library)

If you are new, you can take [Blizzard](https://codeberg.org/Alexander88207/Suyimazu/src/branch/main/Library/Blizzard) or other scripts as an example and edit them to your wishes. If you think your script is ok then you submit it via pull request or issue. I will then look over it and make changes (if necessary) :smiley: 

I will create soon a proper how to.

- Submit new suggestions, your opinion, rating etc..

I am happy about every new idea or suggestion for improvement :cake:
