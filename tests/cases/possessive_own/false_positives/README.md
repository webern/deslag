The clause swallowed ccgo's and libc's own not-implemented diagnostics. It also
swallowed the users' or clients' own messages, and ccgo's, libc's, and rust's own
errors.

It's own file is a typo, and that's own thing is not a noun. There's own
sense in the plan, and who's own turn it is stays open. Let's own the build.
What's own, here's own, where's own and how's own are not nouns either.
He's own and she's own are not nouns, and one's own does not count.

Each package keeps its own config. They keep their own files. Use your own
server, or bring your own. Every worker has its own process. I read my own
notes, and we read our own logs. He reads his own mail and she reads her own.
The base image pins its clock to its own build date.

The place of its own is a room of one's own. A cache can hold its own. A replica
runs on its own, and that is fine.

The repo owns its config. Its owner reads it. Its ownership is clear.
